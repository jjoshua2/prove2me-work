# From actual finite hulls to original-edge coordinate routes

This packet closes the geometric adapter left explicit in accepted #283. Its public target takes only a finite real generator set C and two actual extreme points of its convex hull. It does not take a neighbor relation, a face list, one-edge improvement, connectivity or a short path as premises. The public edges are nondegenerate whole Mathlib IsExtreme segments in the ORIGINAL hull. The local construction proves IsExposed in each retained face; transitively transferring extremality proves the public ordinary-edge property. A separate assertion that every public edge is exposed in the ambient original hull is not included in this target.

First separate a requested actual vertex u from the hull of every other generator. Negating the strict exposing functional gives h(x-u)>0 for every generator x different from u. A better feasible objective value supplies a better generator a. For every pair of nonzero displacements define the normalized contrast h(x-u)*(z-u)-h(z-u)*(x-u). Apply the accepted finite regularization theorem to these contrasts, preserving all nonzero objective signs while resolving every nonzero contrast.

Maximize g(x-u)/h(x-u), then take the farthest generator among the ties. The support q=g-M*h is nonpositive on every generator displacement. A zero q value forces a zero normalized contrast with the chosen endpoint, so every tied generator lies on its forward segment, with parameter between zero and one. Convexity and equality in a support bound prove that the ENTIRE maximizing hull slice is this segment. Its endpoint is an actual extreme point, not just a listed point. Sign preservation on the contrast with the original better generator proves strict improvement for the ORIGINAL objective. No simplicity, inverse, rank or adjacent-neighbor oracle is supplied.

Next prove that filtering any finite generator set at an attained linear maximum yields the entire exposed face. The hull of the actual vertices equals the original hull: extreme points lie among the finite generators, so the closure in Krein--Milman can be removed using the closedness of a finite hull. Enumerate precisely those actual vertices, let adjacency mean a distinct pair whose whole segment is extreme in P, and let admissible finite sets have hull extreme in P. Coordinate minimum/maximum closure and single-edge improvement are now proved geometrically. Transitivity transfers each retained-face edge to P.

The unchanged accepted coordinate rank/descent/ascent construction then joins two fronts through a common coordinate-extreme face, charging at most K_j-1 edges jointly for coordinate j. Induction fixes coordinates until the endpoints coincide. Mapping the indexed sequence back gives actual original vertices and ordinary edges, with length at most sum_j(K_j-1). Coordinates are not nonlinearly rounded; only their finite order ranks are used for counting.

The internal zero_one_routes corollary proves the concrete bound d for the hull of ANY supplied finite family of 0/1 points, not merely the full cube and not assuming its graph. It follows because every actual vertex is a generator and each coordinate image has at most two values.
