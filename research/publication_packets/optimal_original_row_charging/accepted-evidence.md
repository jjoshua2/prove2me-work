target v and a GIVEN finite family of nondegenerate original exposed-edge

    I = target-slack original rows,

    F(J) = {t : S_t is contained in J}.

The accepted geometric witness derives S_t.Nonempty. The theorem constructs an
assignment attaining the least possible maximum row load K. It proves K<=N,

    an allowed capacity-k assignment exists

    k*|J| < |F(J)| <= K*|J|.
