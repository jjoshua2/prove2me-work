# Order-only legality for every original moment vertex

This is the classical Gale-evenness criterion, not a claim of historical novelty. The accepted #299 result enumerates all original vertices from the monic root polynomial and a normalized nodal-sign filter. The new proof replaces that numerical filter by a condition on the ORIGINAL label order only. It is separate from #300's restricted adjacent-pair route and #301's all-endpoint finite-ascent proof, neither of which is edited or resubmitted.

For two unselected labels i<j, multiply q_S(a_i) and q_S(a_j). A factor (a_i-a_s)(a_j-a_s) is negative exactly when i<s<j; otherwise it is positive, and none vanishes. A finite-product induction proves that the product is positive exactly when the number of its negative factors is even. Thus the two evaluations have the same nonzero sign exactly when the selected roots in their gap have even cardinality. This is proved for arbitrary strictly increasing real spacings, not inferred from rational examples or a pre-assumed root pairing.

Because |S|=d<m, some unselected label exists. The parity condition makes every nonzero nodal value have that same sign. Its full-label mean is consequently nonzero, and dividing every nodal value by that mean makes it nonnegative. Conversely, a nonzero mean and nonnegative normalized nodal values make the product of any two nonzero evaluations positive, giving the even-gap condition. The proof explicitly allows a NEGATIVE mean; that regime includes valid wrap-around vertices and cannot be discarded.
