## Exact consequence and limits

For fixed B,G, one C>0 works for every right side t and every nonnegative
x satisfying Bx<=t. A representative y preserves Gy=Gx, every original zero
coordinate and every tight resource row, with mass at most C*(norm(t)+norm(Gx)).
A bounded image therefore has exactly the same image after one finite mass cap;
the capped coefficient set is compact. Neither original coefficient boundedness,
coverage, injectivity, nonemptiness nor a supplied conic decomposition is assumed.
The constant may depend arbitrarily on the coefficients; no dimension-only
conditioning or efficient cap-computation claim is made.
