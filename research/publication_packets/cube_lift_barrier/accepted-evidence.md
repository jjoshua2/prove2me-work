## What is proved, and what remains assumed

For Z containing cube+Q and representing all nonempty Boolean generators,
EVERY compatible pair of extreme lifts above opposite cube corners requires
at least2^d-1 nondegenerate whole exposed Z edges. The proof derives endpoint
saturation in every coefficient representation, proves Boolean directions
nonparallel, and injects the nonempty subsets into actual changing steps.
No objective, saturation, graph, per-edge direction oracle or cost is assumed.

Containment, generator representation and endpoint extremality remain explicit.
Compatible lifts are not constructed here. Q need not be convex or compact.
The all-subset label map requires a nonempty generator index type even for d0.
The canonical pair-completion translation is a written application, not an
extra formal theorem. This extends the project's prior existential antipodal
obstruction to every compatible lift choice for specified original endpoints.

It does NOT transfer a lower bound to the cube or refute Polynomial Hirsch.
It does not obstruct smaller completions or accounting only for nonstationary
contracted steps. These distinctions are borne out by the preserved tests.
Read research/CUBE_LIFT_BARRIER_HANDOFF.md for the next quantitative boundary.
