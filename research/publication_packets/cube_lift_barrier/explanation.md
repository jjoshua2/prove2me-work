# Every compatible lift, not just a globally distant pair

The accepted #317 result constructs opposite zonotope vertices which require many original edges. It leaves a logically distinct possibility: perhaps better choices of compatible sum endpoints make the canonical completion useful for a requested original pair. This theorem rules out that unweighted shortcut for opposite cube corners whenever the completion contains every nonzero Boolean direction.

Let Z=sum_i[0,w_i]. Assume [0,1]^d+Q is contained in Z and the generator list represents every nonempty Boolean coordinate indicator. Take ANY q0,q1 in Q for which q0 and 1+q1 are extreme in Z. Every genuine exposed-segment walk between them has length at least 2^d-1. No exposing objectives, saturation conditions, or changing-step map are inputs.

The new local geometric lemma applies to EVERY coefficient representation at an extreme point x. If x+t*w_i is feasible for 0<=t<=1, a positive coefficient s_i would allow a feasible backward point by changing that coefficient to zero. Its symmetric forward point is feasible too, contradicting extremality. Therefore s_i=0. With a backward feasible segment, the analogous coefficient replacement to one forces s_i=1. At the lower cube lift every Boolean direction can move forward, and at the upper cube lift every such direction can move backward. Thus every selected nonempty-subset coefficient must change from zero to one along any proposed route.

Two nonempty zero-one indicator vectors are scalar multiples only if their supports agree: an occupied coordinate forces the scalar to be one, after which every coordinate agrees. On a nondegenerate whole exposed zonotope edge, the accepted changed_indices_parallel theorem forces any two changing nonzero generator coordinates into the same direction, even for nonunique coefficient representations. Assign one changing step to each nonempty subset. The assignment is injective. The exact number of nonempty subsets is 2^d-1, giving the claimed inequality without a supplied cardinality oracle.

The actual translated-cube inclusion, Boolean generators and endpoint extremality remain explicit. Equality with a Minkowski sum, convexity or compactness of Q are unnecessary. Compatible lifts are quantified universally but their existence is not asserted by this packet. The index map is defined also on the empty subset (unused), so an empty generator type is not covered even in d=0; the d=0 inequality is trivial when the structural inputs exist. This restriction does not affect the stated canonical completion application.

For the #311 all-ordered-cube-pair segment construction, subtracting the fixed sum of starting endpoints gives the coefficient zonotope with generators b-a. Each nonempty Boolean vector occurs with a=0 and b its indicator. The known cube-summand decomposition translates to cube+Q inside that Z. Consequently the obstruction concerns every compatible lift above the specified opposite cube vertices, not only a pair chosen to realize global diameter. This translation/application is a written use of existing construction results, not a second formal theorem here.

The original cube still has a d-edge route between these vertices. Many completion steps can be stationary after Minkowski contraction, and the theorem does NOT prohibit bounding only nonstationary original steps. Nor does it obstruct smaller completions: the cube itself has d generators. It refutes neither Polynomial Hirsch nor a universal completion conjecture. It shows that this rich direction inventory cannot be repaired merely by choosing better lifts while continuing to charge all completion edges. Classical contraction results are credited to Deza--Pournin (arXiv1806.07643,2019); no historical novelty or best general diameter claim is made.
