# The full original-geometric all-endpoint composition

For ordered distinct real parameters a and d<m, use ALL m mean-centered moment inequalities in the ORIGINAL d-dimensional space. The source and target are arbitrary actual extreme points, not selected special vertices. The theorem constructs a walk of at most 2*(m-d)+1 nondegenerate exposed original segments. Every visited point is an actual extreme point, and each segment equals the WHOLE feasible equality slice of its common original tight rows.

The complete numeric catalogue reconstructs every endpoint from its d tight labels using a monic root polynomial and its full-label mean. The unchanged MomentEvenGaps proof identifies exactly which label sets pass the scalar filter: Gale evenness. It derives the nonzero mean and permits BOTH mean signs. The accepted selected-set route then constructs all intermediate legal d-subsets in at most 2*(m-d)+1 single-label exchanges. The parity catalogue gives an actual feasible extreme point for every such set, with exactly those original labels tight. Consequently consecutive points share exactly d-1 tight rows. The accepted common-row theorem identifies the entire slice with their nondegenerate exposed segment. Injectivity of the exact active-set correspondence prevents a nontrivial label exchange from collapsing to a stationary point.

Nothing resembling a feasible catalogue, parity condition, rank inverse, legal intermediate sequence or bounded original-edge route is assumed. These are derived from the explicit inequalities and arbitrary endpoint extremality. The endpoint label sets need not be consecutive or separated adjacent pairs. Zero and odd dimensions, equal endpoints and all strictly positive parameter gaps are covered. The represented row count m is not silently replaced by an asserted irredundant facet count in degenerate dimensions.
