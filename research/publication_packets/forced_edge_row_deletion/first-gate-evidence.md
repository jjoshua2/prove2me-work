## Concrete mathematics and remaining mission gap

all target-tight rows plus J to form Q. For an original exposed nondegenerate
edge e avoiding v whose entire target-slack common-tight label set is in J,
the written candidate constructs E(e)=Q intersect affline(e). It derives whole
exposure, e subset E(e), v outside E(e), and exact recovery P intersect E(e)=e.
Thus equal relaxed carriers imply equal original geometric edge sets.

The active-equation line is derived using finite two-sided perturbations at
the midpoint and the original exposing functional. It is not an active-rank
hypothesis. The remaining local compilation error lies in constructing the
summed-row linear functional, not in that independently audited line argument.

number or congestion. Original endpoints need NOT remain relaxed vertices,
and carriers may be longer segments or rays. Repeated occurrences are not made
geometrically distinct. The theorem is sufficient, not an iff survival test.

whole exposed segments, non-target distinct endpoints and target extremality
remain explicit; dimension is arbitrary, with no boundedness, full dimension,
finite-hull, simplicity or vertex-catalogue premise.
