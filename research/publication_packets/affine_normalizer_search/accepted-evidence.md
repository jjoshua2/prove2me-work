denominator positive at all points of C. Whenever a subsystem's explicit linear

Infeasible subsystems get a positive fallback, not a claimed feasible solution.

Every arbitrary positive affine denominator a+D(x), with REAL coefficients,
has all its ratio coincidences preserved by some catalogue member. Its spectrum
therefore cannot be smaller than that member's spectrum. A single catalogue
entry attains the minimum spectrum cardinality over ALL positive affine
denominators, not merely a supplied guess list or a sampled search.

The proof normalizes at u, linearizes each ratio equality in d slope variables,
and derives at most d original contrast tests spanning all ties. Difference-
of-solutions kernel reasoning preserves every old equality. Finite choice and
minimization close the global optimum theorem. Signed/zero numerator data,
nonspanning C, dimension0 and arbitrarily small positive denominators remain.

hypothesis is supplied.

This is a classical-choice existence/completeness theorem, NOT an extracted
strict-LP solver. Enumeration may be exponential in d and in the vertex
