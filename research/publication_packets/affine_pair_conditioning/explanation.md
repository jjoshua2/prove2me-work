# Uniform metric barrier and exact attainment

    X(X+2eZ+e^2Y)-D=(Z+eX)^2+(1-e^2)X(X-Y)

shows that the first squared sine is at most e^2. If Y>=X, interchange X and Y. Denominators are strictly positive because

    X(X+2eZ+e^2Y)=(X+eZ)^2+e^2 D.
