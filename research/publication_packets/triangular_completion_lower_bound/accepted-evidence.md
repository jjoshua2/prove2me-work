    0 <= x_n <= 1;
    e*x_(i+1) <= x_i <= 1-e*x_(i+1), for i<n.

These are exactly 2(n+1) DISPLAYED original inequalities. For every nonempty compact
Q and every ACTUAL equality P+Q=sum_i[0,w_i], the proof derives 2^n<=m and constructs
opposite extreme completion vertices u,v such that every walk through nondegenerate

not required. The whole finite-hull representation, 2^n genuine original exposed
edges and their distinct unoriented directions are proved, not assumed as oracles.

extension and asymptotic domination theorem remain outside this packet.
