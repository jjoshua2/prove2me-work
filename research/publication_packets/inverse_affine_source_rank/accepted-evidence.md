strictly positive on z-v for every non-target point of C. For z different from v,
define inverse heights ell_D(z)=(1+D(z-v))/h(z-v). These are affine functions of
unconstrained D. The theorem proves that shifting D by c*h translates all heights
by c, preserves the distinct upper count relative to x, and can make the affine
denominator positive on all C.
