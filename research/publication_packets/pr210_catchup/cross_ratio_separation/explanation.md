# Cross-ratio separation obstruction

Because `delta ≤ s` and `delta ≤ t` with all quantities nonnegative, `delta^2 ≤ s*t`. The factors `u` and `v` lie in `[0,1]`, so `u*v ≤ 1`; multiplying by nonnegative `epsilon` gives `epsilon*u*v ≤ epsilon`. The supplied product identity `s*t = epsilon*u*v` therefore implies `delta^2 ≤ epsilon`. In the geometric application, the normalized determinant cross-ratio supplies the product identity, so at least one relevant line separation must collapse when `epsilon` is small.
