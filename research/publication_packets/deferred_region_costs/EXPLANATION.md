Choose a shortest region walk using graph distance. A nonconsecutive chord would shorten it, so its support is chordless and duplicate-free. Induct on that fixed walk, choosing one shared point at each consecutive region intersection. This constructs the list of portal pairs using only region geometry. After those choices, take any routing graph and costs; concatenate the walks supplied for the listed pairs. Walk lengths add, giving the sum bound. The zero-edge region walk consists of one pair in a single region, and zero-cost local walks are allowed.

This strengthens the usable quantifier order of the earlier repair interfaces: local bounds for every pair in every region are no longer prerequisites for obtaining the selected path. It does not supply those local bounds or solve the few-used-cuts, high-excess case.
