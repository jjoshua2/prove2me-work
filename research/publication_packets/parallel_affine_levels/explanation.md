# Parallel displacements force coordinate levels

The public target formalizes the finite counting step of the already-written all-affine level obstruction in PR #276. It does not resubmit the accepted affine-angle theorem from #275, and does not assert the entire Klee--Minty family or Polynomial Hirsch as its target.

First prove a scalar lemma. With a_i,b_i in a finite level set L, b_i-a_i=lambda_i*c, c nonzero, lambda_i positive and pairwise distinct, the map i -> (a_i,b_i) is injective. Its reversed map is also injective. Their images are disjoint: a forward pair equal to a reversed pair would force (lambda_i+lambda_j)*c=0, contradicting positivity and c nonzero. No assumption that c is positive is needed. Each pair has unequal entries, so both images lie in L.offDiag. Its cardinality is |L|*(|L|-1), proving 2*card(I)<=|L|*(|L|-1).

For the geometric interface, injectivity of T and g nonzero imply T g is not the zero function. Choose a coordinate where it does not vanish. Apply that coordinate to each vector displacement identity, cancel the translation, and invoke the scalar result. The argument uses arbitrary real vector spaces and finite-dimensional injective affine images; it is not based on chart sampling or a finite chart catalogue.

Remaining hypotheses are explicit: endpoint membership in a finite set, a common nonzero direction, positive distinct displacement coefficients, and injectivity of the linear part. General projected extensions and noninjective maps are outside the theorem. No original-edge adjacency is presumed or concluded. Instantiating the lemma with the separately justified 2^(d-1) parallel Klee--Minty edges yields the project's written exponential coordinate-level obstruction, but that construction is not smuggled into the Lean type.
