sum_j (number of distinct ACTUAL vertex coordinate-j values minus one).

edge existence are derived. There is no supplied graph, rank, neighbor, face
catalogue, connectivity or short-route oracle. Redundant/interior generators,
nonsimple/lower-dimensional hulls, d=0 and equal endpoints are allowed.
