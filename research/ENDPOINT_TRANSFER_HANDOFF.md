## Main result and actual assumptions

makes each summand change once directly from its source maximizing coordinate
to its target maximizing coordinate. All equal ordered endpoint pairs switch
on one whole exposed original edge. Therefore length equals the number of
nonzero transfer groups, at most binom(n,2), with every common exposed face
preserved. This is not a new classical generalized-permutahedron bound; it is
an exact path-local interface and original-H implementation of the mechanism.

The H wrapper checks a connected building set, positive weights, and its full
canonical H system. It extracts exposing orders from actual endpoint bases.
It does not discover hidden coordinate charts or a presentation of arbitrary
H input. Deleting the last coordinate is an affine bijection from sum x=T,
not extension projection. Every edge gets both whole-support and original
inverse/maximal-step checks.

The exact |G| path is shortest only among routes whose individual summands
never use choices outside their two endpoint choices. Do not erase that
restriction. Interval nestohedra provide binom(n,2) returned versus n-1 shortest
edges; n16 is120 versus15. Keep those adverse fixtures.
