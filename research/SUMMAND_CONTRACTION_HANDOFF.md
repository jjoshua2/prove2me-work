## Closed generic geometric interface

For convex P,Q in R^d and a supplied actual extreme-point/exposed-edge walk
z_0,...,z_N of their Minkowski sum, derive unique extreme decompositions
z_i=a_i+b_i. Every component segment is a WHOLE exposed factor face, possibly a
singleton. At each sum edge with displacement D, the component displacements are
alpha*D and(1-alpha)*D, where0<=alpha<=1. Remove stationary component transitions
to obtain actual exposed and extreme edge walks in each factor, with individual
lengths L_P<=N and L_Q<=N. Their sum need not be<=N.

Cross-sum midpoint extremality proves uniqueness and component extrema. For an
arbitrary component maximizer x of a sum edge, both x+q0 and x+q1 belong to the
whole sum segment. Their parameters satisfy t=s+1-alpha, forcing0<=s<=alpha.
This bounds the entire component support face, not just its endpoints. Convexity
supplies the reverse inclusion, including collapsed factors. The accepted32-line
schedule-compression proof from #306 is reused unchanged under this namespace.

The theorem does not assume a component catalogue/map, uniqueness, support-face
or rank witness, direction split, factor graph or component route. It does assume
genuine convex Minkowski structure and the original sum walk. Boundedness,
closedness, finite hulls, simplicity and full dimension are unnecessary. Zero-length
and zero-dimensional cases are retained. No unrestricted Polynomial Hirsch,
shortestness, arbitrary-projection transport or historical novelty is claimed.
