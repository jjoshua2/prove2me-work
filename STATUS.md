### Exact safe operation and residual count

A higher defect is a minimal nonface with at least three labels. For a FACE
edge E={u,v} subdivided with z, all new minimal nonfaces are the minimal members
of {E}, old N not containing E, and {z} union(N minus E) for old N meeting E.
This is proved by membership and checked by explicit simplex subdivision.

If u and v have the SAME NONEMPTY incidence in all higher defects, every high
nonface contains both or neither. Replace the pair by z in the former and drop
descendants becoming pairs; all other high defects stay unchanged. No splitting
occurs. Old u,v remain vertices of the subdivision; original facets are not
removed or identified. Every safe step decreases higher-defect support.

With q initial higher defects, k labels in their union, t safe moves and residual
higher support h, the exact conclusions are

    t<=k-h, q'<=q, h<=2^q'-1<=2^q-1.

The terminal nonempty incidence signatures are distinct. Finish with #260's
single residual-block refinement. If f_B is the number of its nonempty faces,

    M=m+t-h+f_B,  diameter(original)<=M-d=e+t+f_B-h.

The bound is linear in m for FIXED q with a potentially doubly exponential
q-dependent additive constant; it is NOT a universal polynomial estimate.
No small q or small residual core is assumed for arbitrary polytopes. The exact
M is often more useful than the loose worst case. Disjoint high supports and
common-core sunflowers admit explicit all-safe elimination schedules; at most
two higher defects always resolve with the given formulas.

Each stellar carrier map sends adjacent refined maximal simplices to equal
or adjacent old ones; composing all maps and deleting stationary steps gives
actual original edges. The code checks every intermediate adjacency and the
final original-H inverse/maximum-step identities. Compatible lifts preserve
all original facets common to the endpoints. Refined routes need not be raw
combinatorial segments in the original complex and can therefore leave that
published exponentially constrained route family.
